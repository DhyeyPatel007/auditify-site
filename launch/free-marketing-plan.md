# Auditify — Zero-Budget Marketing Plan (First 14 Days)

Product: website-audit SaaS. Free scan → $24 report → $19/mo monitoring → $109/mo agency.
Voice: quiet, confident, precise. Hero: "A website audit you'll actually read."
Audience: small businesses, agencies, developers in the US/UK/Canada.
Budget: ₹0. Founder in India — post when the US/UK is awake (IST 6pm–1am window).

## The core bets

1. **The free scan IS the marketing.** Every scan is a shareable artifact. The product demos itself.
2. **Teardowns, not ads.** Public audits of real sites teach, entertain, and prove the tool in one post.
3. **Direct outreach with personalized free audits** is the highest-ROI hour you have. Nothing beats it early.
4. **Build in public where makers hang out** (X, Indie Hackers) — credibility compounds.
5. **Earn the first 10 customers by hand.** Automate nothing until a human process works.

## Channels — what to do on each

**X/Twitter** (primary daily channel)
- 1–2 posts/day. Mix: teardown threads, build-in-public updates ("shipped X", "learned Y from 50 audits"), one-line audit tips.
- Post in IST evening so US/UK sees it morning.
- Reply to 5 relevant accounts/day (SEO folks, indie hackers, agency owners) — be useful, not salesy.

**LinkedIn** (business owners + agencies live here)
- 3–4 posts/week. Longer teardowns work best: "I audited 10 dental clinic websites. 8 had the same 3 problems."
- Connect with agency owners/small-biz marketers; DM the teardown playbook (below), don't pitch in the post.

**Indie Hackers**
- One launch post (week 1), then milestone posts ("first $100", "what 200 free audits taught me"). Comment genuinely on 3 posts/week.

**Discord** (your strongest channel — use it)
- Webdev/SEO/maker servers. Answer questions, drop the tool ONLY when someone asks "how do I check X?" — never cold-drop links.

**Reddit** — ⚠️ your account is banned. Do NOT create a throwaway to spam links; that's how accounts die permanently. Either rebuild one account slowly with genuine participation (no links for 30 days) or skip Reddit entirely for now. X + LinkedIn + IH cover the same ground.

**Hacker News** — one "Show HN" when the tool is stable and you have a good story ("I audited 500 sites, here's what I learned"). High variance; one shot, don't repost.

**Product Hunt** — one launch day. Needs ~1 week prep: hunter/teaser post, first-comment story, be online all day replying. Schedule for week 2–3, not day 1.

**SEO (slow burn, start now)** — one plain-English explainer per week on the site blog: "What is HSTS, in plain English", "Why your images are slow", etc. Each post = one audit check explained = ranks for real queries over months. The tool pages themselves rank for "free website audit" long-tail eventually.

**Direct outreach** (the money channel — see next-steps.md #6)
- 10–15 personalized free audits/day via email/LinkedIn DM. The script is in next-steps.md.

## The teardown playbook (your repeatable content engine)

1. **Find targets:** Google `"[trade]" "[US/UK city]"` — dentists, lawyers, realtors, Shopify stores, SaaS startups. Run each through your own scanner first; pick ones with interesting, fixable findings (not disasters, not perfect scores).
2. **Private first:** email/DM the owner the full report, free, no ask. "Thought you'd want to see this." Ask: "Mind if I share the interesting bits publicly? I'll keep it kind."
3. **Public post** (with permission, or anonymized as "a Shopify store"): "I audited [X]. 3 things quietly hurting them: ① … ② … ③ …" + screenshots + what each issue costs them + "Full scan is free: [link]".
4. **Rules:** never punch down on tiny struggling businesses publicly. Never shame — teach. Always end with the tool link and "run your own free scan."
5. **Cadence:** one teardown every 2–3 days. Quality > quantity — a great teardown outperforms five lazy ones.

## 14-day calendar

**Day 1 — Foundation**
- Set up/polish X account (bio: "I built Auditify — a website audit you'll actually read. Free scan ↓"). Polish LinkedIn headline.
- Write the Indie Hackers launch post (story: why readable audits, what you built, free scan link).
- Build a target list of 50 sites for teardowns/outreach (spreadsheet: site, owner name, email/LinkedIn, findings).

**Day 2 — Launch online**
- Post Indie Hackers launch. X thread: "I launched Auditify today — a website audit you'll actually read. Here's what it does and why I built it 🧵" (5–7 tweets, end with link).
- Run 15 target sites through the scanner; send the first 5 private free-audit emails.

**Day 3 — First teardown**
- Publish teardown #1 (X thread + LinkedIn post, same content adapted).
- 10 more private outreach emails. Reply to every IH comment.

**Day 4 — Outreach**
- 15 private outreach emails. X: one audit tip post ("Your images are probably 3x heavier than they need to be. Here's the 2-min fix:").
- Start Product Hunt prep (draft tagline, first comment, screenshots).

**Day 5 — Teardown #2**
- Publish teardown #2. Follow up on day 2–3 outreach non-responders (one polite bump).
- LinkedIn: reshare teardown #1 with a business-owner angle ("what this costs you in customers").

**Day 6 — Outreach + learn**
- 15 outreach emails. X: build-in-public post ("50 free audits sent this week. The #1 most common issue: ___").
- Read all concierge replies; note confusion points.

**Day 7 — Weekly review (30 min)**
- Numbers: scans, emails, outreach sent/replied, any revenue. What worked?
- Rest. (Burnout kills more startups than competitors.)

**Day 8 — Teardown #3 + HN**
- Publish teardown #3. If the tool is stable and you have an interesting story, post Show HN in the morning IST (evening US = good HN time is actually US morning → post ~6:30pm IST).
- 10 outreach emails.

**Day 9 — Product Hunt**
- Launch on Product Hunt. Be online all day replying to comments. X + LinkedIn + Discord: "we're live on PH today."
- No outreach today — all energy on PH.

**Day 10 — Follow-ups**
- 15 outreach emails (new batch). Bump PH commenters with thanks.
- X: "What launching on Product Hunt taught me" post.

**Day 11 — Teardown #4**
- Publish teardown #4 (make this one an agency or SaaS — speak to buyers with budget).
- Start first SEO explainer post ("What is HSTS, in plain English").

**Day 12 — Outreach + SEO**
- 15 outreach emails. Publish the SEO explainer on the site.
- LinkedIn: teardown #4 adapted.

**Day 13 — Double down**
- Look at 12 days of data: which channel brought scans? replies? money? Do more of that, less of the rest.
- X: milestone post with real numbers ("13 days, X scans, Y emails, Z customers").

**Day 14 — Review + plan**
- Full weekly review. Write down: best channel, best teardown, top 3 user confusions, revenue.
- Plan week 3–4 using next-steps.md. Keep the daily rhythm: outreach + 1 post + 1 teardown every 2–3 days.

## What NOT to do

- **No link spam** — on Reddit, Discord, IH, anywhere. One spam flag costs more than 100 posts earn.
- **No fake proof** — no invented testimonials, no "trusted by" logos, no inflated user counts. Ever.
- **No bought followers / engagement pods** — dead numbers that kill reach.
- **No mass DMs** — 15 thoughtful emails beat 500 copy-pastes.
- **No discounting the $24** — it anchors the value. Discount only in a deliberate, time-boxed experiment.
- **No AppSumo-style lifetime deal** — not until you have real traction and know your costs.
- **No building features instead of outreach** — if you're coding in week 2, you're hiding.
- **No public shaming teardowns** — teach, don't humiliate. Permission first when naming names.
