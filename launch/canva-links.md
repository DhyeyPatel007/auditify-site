## Note on size
Both designs came out at 1080×1350 (4:5 portrait) rather than 1080×1080 square — Canva resolved "Instagram Post" to portrait. 4:5 portrait actually gets more feed real estate on Instagram/Facebook than square; if square is required, use Design → Resize in the Canva editor (link above).
